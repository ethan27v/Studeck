Ambient sounds for the Study page
================================

All four recordings are by Joseph SARDIN - BigSoundBank.com, released under
CC0 1.0 (public domain): https://bigsoundbank.com/licenses.html

They were cut into seamless loops (a few seconds crossfaded where each loop
wraps around), evened out in volume and saved as Opus at 63 kbps.

  rain.ogg   "Summer Rain on Terrace"         https://bigsoundbank.com/summer-rain-on-terrace-s1019.html
  waves.ogg  "Small Waves Facing the Ocean"   https://bigsoundbank.com/small-waves-facing-the-ocean-s1046.html
  fire.ogg   "Fireplace #3"                   https://bigsoundbank.com/fireplace-3-s2855.html
  cafe.ogg   "Coffee Shop at the Capucins"    https://bigsoundbank.com/coffee-shop-at-the-capucins-s2561.html

"Noise" (brown noise) isn't a file: the dashboard makes it as it plays.
